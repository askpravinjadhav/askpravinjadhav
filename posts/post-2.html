Five years of freelance work

From 2021 to 2025 I was self-employed, working as a freelance graphic and UI/UX designer. There was no sales team and no inbound pipeline — I built the client base myself, through cold calling and direct outreach, qualifying leads and running the first conversation on my own.

A lot of that work happened before any design started. Owners often couldn't specify what they actually wanted — the job was to sit with that, ask the right questions, and turn a vague conversation into a real scope, timeline and price.

Then came the proposals and quotations, the pricing objections, and eventually closing and delivering the work. Looking back, the freelance years were as much sales as they were design — that mix is what carried forward into everything after.
